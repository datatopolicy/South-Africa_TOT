Data to Policy (D2P)
Training of Trainers Workshop

November 16 - 19, 2026 | Muldersdrift, Gauteng, South Africa

Strengthening mentor capacity in economic evaluation and policy translation across South Africa

Welcome to the D2P TOT Workshop

The Data to Policy (D2P) program, developed by Vital Strategies, the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), and the CDC Foundation as part of the Bloomberg Philanthropies Data for Health Initiative, aims to bridge the data-policy gap through team-based training and mentoring of health policy professionals in low- to middle-income countries.

Workshop Objectives

By the end of the TOT workshop, participants will be able to:

  • Explain the components of D2P
  • Define their roles as facilitators and mentors
  • Apply instructional skills to facilitate D2P courses
  • Respond to common facilitation challenges
  • Conduct end-of-day participant debriefs
  • Mentor D2P participants through all phases of the D2P course
  • Handle mentoring challenges related to developing D2P skills

Program Background

D2P participants develop data-driven policy briefs and recommendations that respond to government health priorities. The program employs several types of analysis—including economic evaluation of policy options—to inform policy recommendations.

To ensure the program's long-term sustainability, the CDC Foundation is hosting this Training of Trainers (TOT) workshop for South Africa, cultivating a pool of skilled in-country mentors and facilitators capable of independently leading future D2P cohorts.

In South Africa, the programme has built a national mentor network to support applied economic evaluation and policy translation. This workshop standardises mentor capacity where it matters most — economic evaluation (Unit 2) and structured policy translation — so that every cohort receives the same high-quality, consistent mentoring.

Participating Countries

This workshop brings together 15 mentors from South Africa's national D2P mentor network, including participants from out of province.

One Case Study, Start to Finish

The workshop follows a single integrated syphilis case study from problem framing through to policy translation — evidence, costing, decision tree, Markov model, sensitivity analysis and validation, then policy recommendations. Mentors work in groups throughout, refining the tools and aligning how the content is taught and mentored.

Tools You'll Use

Amua for model development; the Excel-based syphilis case study and cost inventory worksheet; gender analysis worksheets; and the Tufts CEA Registry for published cost-effectiveness evidence.

Beyond the Workshop

Standardised tools and mentorship approaches from this TOT feed into D2P Units 2A and 2B, and D2P methods are being integrated into the Intermediate Field Epidemiology Training Programme (iFETP) — building on a pilot in Mpumalanga Province, with the next cohort planned in KwaZulu-Natal.

Eligibility & Nomination

Ideal candidates are graduates of the D2P course, selected by their local government or nominated by DI country coordinators based on performance and demonstrated interest in facilitation and mentoring. Participants should be mid-level public health professionals from Ministries of Health, public health institutes, or national universities, with expertise in data analysis, program planning, or health policy development.

Eligibility criteria include:

  • Prior completion of a D2P course or equivalent experience in data-driven policy work
  • Commitment to facilitating or mentoring future D2P courses in their country
  • Endorsement from the Ministry of Health or a relevant government body
  • No prior participation in any D2P TOT workshop
  • No previous experience as a mentor or facilitator in D2P cohorts
  • Availability for the full workshop duration and follow-up activities

Participants are nominated based on performance and demonstrated interest in facilitation and mentoring, prioritizing those who can contribute to building a sustainable cadre of trainers.
