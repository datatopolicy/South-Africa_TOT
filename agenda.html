Workshop Agenda

November 16 - 19, 2026 | Riverstone Lodge, Muldersdrift, Gauteng, South Africa

Overview

The 4-day Training of Trainers workshop focuses on interactive learning, presentations, and mentoring practice. Each session includes opportunities for hands-on application, feedback, and preparation for real-world D2P implementation.

Please note: The schedule below is subject to change. Session times below follow the standard TOT schedule — please check the D2P ToT WhatsApp group for updates.

Daily Schedule:

  • Days 1-3 (November 16-18): approx. 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM (Day 1 ends ~4:00 PM; Days 2-3 end ~3:30 PM)
  • Day 4 (November 19): approx. 9:00 AM - 1:30 PM
  • Includes morning and afternoon tea breaks and a midday lunch break each day
